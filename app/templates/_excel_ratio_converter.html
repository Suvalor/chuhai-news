Excel 怎么按比例分摊金额？

复制表格数据行，在第一列用斜杠分隔姓名，其余列填金额或其他数值。输入小数比例，例如 0.6 表示 60%。生成汇总后，同名记录按列相加，结果可以粘贴回 Excel。

60/40 分账示例

第一列填 张三/李四，第二列填 1000。把张三的比例改为 0.6，李四自动分到 0.4，结果分别是 600 和 400。

How do you split amounts proportionally in Excel?

Copy the data rows. Separate names with slashes in the first column and put numeric values in the other columns. Enter shares as decimals, such as 0.6 for 60%. The converter sums matching names by column and lets you paste the results back into Excel.

Example of a 60/40 split

Put Alice/Bob in the first column and 1000 in the second. Set Alice’s share to 0.6. Bob’s share becomes 0.4, giving totals of 600 and 400.

Is my spreadsheet data uploaded?

Calculation runs in your browser. Pasted cell contents never go to the site’s server. Copy a range of cells from Excel so the converter can read the pasted values and text.
